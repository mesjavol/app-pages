Subscout: keep track of every subscription, without signing up for another one

Subscout is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that keeps all your subscriptions in one place: what each one costs, when it renews and what you could stop paying for. It reminds you before every charge and before a free trial ends.

It's free to download and try for 14 days. Then one purchase ($14.99 / 14,99 € / 149 kr) unlocks it for life on all your Apple devices. No subscription.

What makes it different

  • One purchase, never a subscription.
  • No bank login, no account. You add what you pay for yourself.
  • Private: no ads, no tracking, no third-party code. Data stays on the device and syncs through the user's own iCloud. The App Store privacy label says "Data Not Collected".
  • A real Mac app, with this month's total in the menu bar.

Features in short

Reminders before every renewal, free-trial tracking, automatic price-increase notes, a calendar of upcoming charges, widgets, a monthly review (keep, pause or cancel), analytics for 3, 6 or 12 months, multiple currencies with the European Central Bank's daily rates, import and export (JSON, CSV).
