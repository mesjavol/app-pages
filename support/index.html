Frequently asked questions

Where is my data? On your device, and in your private iCloud if you are signed in. We can't see it.

Do my devices sync? Yes, between devices signed in with the same Apple Account and with iCloud on. Changes can take a few minutes to arrive. Settings shows the sync state.

I'm not getting reminders. Open Settings, turn on "Remind me before renewals" and allow notifications. Reminders are set up per device. "Send a Test Reminder" checks that they work. If notifications were denied, enable them in the system settings.

The widget is empty. Open the app once; the widget shows what the app last wrote. After that it updates by itself.

How do I back up or move my data? Settings, Data: Export JSON (full backup) or CSV (for spreadsheets). Import JSON on another device.

A total shows several currencies. Each currency has its own total. Turn on conversion in Settings, Currency, to see one combined total (uses the European Central Bank's daily rates). Currencies the app can't recognise (for example a typo like "kr89") stay separate; fix the currency in the subscription.

How do I delete everything? Settings, Data: Delete All Subscriptions.

Does the app cancel subscriptions for me? No. It helps you decide and reminds you; cancelling is done with the service itself.
