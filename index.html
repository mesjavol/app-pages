Questions

Is Subscout itself a subscription?

No. You buy it once and it's yours, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your list syncs through your own iCloud, if you use it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. You add your subscriptions yourself, which takes a few seconds each. Your bank details never come near the app.

Can you see my data?

No. Nothing is sent to the developer. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What if I don't use iCloud?

Subscout works fully on one device. Sign in to iCloud later and your list syncs.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later, iPad with iPadOS 18 or later, and Mac with macOS 15 or later.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Export JSON or CSV any time, and import a JSON backup.
