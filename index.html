Know what you pay for, and when.

Subscout keeps every subscription in one calm place: what each one costs, when it renews, and a reminder before every charge. For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

A quiet look at your money

What it does

Everything in one place

Add streaming, music, news, cloud, gym and anything else, with price, billing cycle and next renewal date. See your total per month and per year.

Never be surprised

A reminder before every renewal, at the time you choose. Track free trials, and see when a price has gone up.

See what's coming

A calendar of upcoming charges. Widgets for your Home Screen, Lock Screen and desktop, and a menu bar icon on the Mac.

Decide once a month

A monthly review takes you through the coming renewals one by one: keep, pause or cancel, with a reminder to do it with the service itself.

Your currencies

Each currency gets its own total. Turn on conversion if you like, and see everything in one currency using the European Central Bank's daily rates.

Yours to keep

Import and export your data (JSON, CSV) whenever you want. Works in English and Swedish.

Your subscriptions are your business

Subscout collects no data. There is no account to create and nothing to sign up for, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code.

Your list lives on your devices and syncs between them through your own private iCloud, which the developer can't read.

The only time the app goes online by itself is if you switch on currency conversion: it then downloads the European Central Bank's public rates file and sends nothing about you or your subscriptions.
